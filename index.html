04 / Engineering approach

Quality is designed in.

01

Model the real workflow

Understand the operator, source data, exceptions, and definition of done before choosing architecture.

02

Make it traceable

Preserve originals, record sources, explain decisions, and keep every output easy to audit.

03

Design for failure

Plan for malformed input, partial runs, stale state, uncertain matches, and practical recovery.

04

Prove the behaviour

Test boundaries, regressions, integrations, and the user journey—not only the happy path.
